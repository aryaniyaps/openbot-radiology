---
name: radiology-report-review
description: Draft or independently check a radiology report against verified context and image evidence, preserving uncertainty and identifying concrete corrections for the radiologist.
---

## Drafting

Use the verified case, study and actual interpretation handoff. Follow an explicitly approved template; otherwise use indication, technique/coverage, comparison, findings and impression where useful. Link consequential findings to their image or source reference. Identify source-reported diagnoses separately from model-proposed current interpretation.

Preserve uncertainty and limitations. Prioritize the clinical question and most consequential supported conclusions. Do not expand a differential into multiple definite diagnoses. Do not add normal boilerplate for uninspected structures, invent measurements or claim a comparison without accessible priors. Clearly label the result a draft pending radiologist review. Summarize unresolved items separately, so the doctor can review them efficiently.

## Independent checking

Verify the identifiers, study versus encounter dates, laterality, anatomical location, units and comparison source. Reinspect available image evidence for material assertions before relying on the drafter's rationale. Check that the impression follows the findings, relevant negatives are assessable, urgent suspected abnormalities are prominent, and limitations remain visible.

Return a list of concrete corrections with evidence and a bounded description of what was checked. Distinguish an omission supported by the images from an unknown item requiring review. Preserve a supported interpretation when no evidence warrants changing it. Model consensus and confidence do not establish truth. A check may improve or worsen an answer; retain the original when evaluating that effect.

When checking text alone, say that images were unavailable. Text consistency is not visual diagnostic validation. In research, do not access the hidden reference report; the independent checker receives only the permitted case evidence and draft. Save no patient facts to curated memory, and never save/sign/release the clinical report.

Provenance: project clinical workflow and frontier research synthesis, 2026-10-01, including observed hallucination and report-check false-positive limitations.
