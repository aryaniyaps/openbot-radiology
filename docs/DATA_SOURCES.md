# Public imaging provenance

The downloadable originals and checksums are recorded privately in `.private/dicom/source-manifest.json`; public selected-series metadata and licensing are in `config/data/public-manifest.json`.

- DX and CT: [COVID-19-NY-SBU](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/covid-19-ny-sbu/), CC BY 4.0.
- MRI: [PROSTATEx](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/prostatex/), CC BY 3.0.
- Ultrasound: [Prostate-MRI-US-Biopsy](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/prostate-mri-us-biopsy/), CC BY 4.0.
- Mammography: [CMMD](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/cmmd/), CC BY 4.0.

Retain collection attribution, linked source citation instructions and license notices when sharing recordings or cases. Replays retain source pixel bytes, SOP classes and geometry. Patient identifiers, name, birth date, sex and accession are synthetic; UIDs are remapped. No real Kauvery patient data was used.
